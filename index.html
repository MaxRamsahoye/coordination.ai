          Coordinate

          A coordinated slowdown needs people across government, industry and the public to act together. Here is where to start.

          1. Tell your representatives

            Legislators are weighing bills on superintelligence and catastrophic risk now. A short, personal message about where you stand carries weight, especially if you ask for a specific action.

            • UK: write to your MP or a peer ↗
            • US: find your senators and representative ↗

          2. Add your name

            Public statements show decision-makers how broad support for coordination is.

            • Statement on Superintelligence ↗
            • Global Call for AI Red Lines ↗
            • Pacing the Frontier (for employees of frontier AI companies) ↗

          3. Join a campaign

            Campaign groups organise briefings, letters and events, and can put you in touch with others near you.

            • ControlAI ↗
            • PauseAI ↗
            • Future of Life Institute ↗

          4. Improve this record

            Every position, statement and incident here needs a source. If you know of one that's missing, or something that's out of date, send it in.
