      Method

      Coordination Console tracks one question: will the actors building and governing frontier AI coordinate to pace its development, or race? It pairs a timeline of events with a map of positions held by individuals and institutions across government, industry, civil society, academia and multilateral bodies.

      Position scale

      Each actor is placed on a five-point scale describing their current stated position on a coordinated slowdown of frontier development.

      Where stated positions and conduct diverge — a lab endorsing a slowdown while shipping faster, a government calling for governance while rejecting restraint — the actor profile carries a tension note rather than averaging the two. Positions reflect public statements and actions as of the dataset date and will change; each is backed by dated evidence.

      Event direction

      Every event is tagged ▲ toward coordination if it moves the world toward shared limits, verification, pacing or binding oversight, ▼ toward racing if it removes constraints or intensifies competition, and ● mixed otherwise. This is an editorial judgement about direction, not about whether the event is good or bad.

      Sources and corrections

      Entries link to primary sources or reputable reporting where available. The data lives in plain files (data/events.js, data/actors.js) so corrections and additions can be proposed as pull requests. Recent entries — especially those from the past few weeks — describe fast-moving stories and should be read alongside the latest reporting.
